Entry for the [The Moon Finally Answers Back](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-the-moon-finally-answers-back-781a7ec1) bounty on make.fun ($2, deadline 28 Nov 2026). The program writes exactly one line to `entry.txt`: the Moon's reply to everyone who has ever stared up at it.

## The line

> Four billion years of wishes, howls, and 3 a.m. promises, and not one of you has ever wished me a good night back.

## Why this line

It answers all three kinds of callers at once: the wishers, the howlers, and the late-night promise-makers. The joke is a polite reversal: humanity spends millennia asking the Moon for things, and the Moon's one complaint is the smallest courtesy imaginable, a good night back. "Good night back" does double work: it is the bedtime phrase humans say to each other, and it is also literally what the Moon owns, the night. The voice is old, wry and a little tired of being wished upon, and the turn lands in the final clause, which flips the wish back onto the wishers.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (mode 0644), after checking it is 1-240 characters long, a single line, and not only whitespace. Set `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` to write somewhere else.
